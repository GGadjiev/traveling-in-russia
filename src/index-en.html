Altai

URL YouTube

Altai is one of the most beautiful places in Russia. First of all, because of the mountains: if you go along the ridge, you will see slopes strewn with pine trees, mountain rivers and lakes. And if you open the windows in the car, you will be able to get acquainted with the invisible miracle of the local places — mountain air. The climate in Altai is temperate, so go here. it is best in summer. So you will see all the variety. local flora and fauna. Wandering through the forests of Altai moose, eagles fly over the ridges, and on the plains Roe deer graze. And the famous manuls — They are also inhabitants of the Altai Territory.
